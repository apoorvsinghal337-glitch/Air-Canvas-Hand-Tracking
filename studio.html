BACKUP BUTTONS (OPTIONAL)

All drawing tools can be selected using hand gestures after camera starts. Backup buttons are here in case tracking is temporarily lost.

FINGER GESTURE GUIDE
☝️ 1 fingerDraw on the whiteboard. In menu: point at a tile and hold to select.
✌️ 2 fingersPause drawing and end current stroke.
3 fingersIndex + middle + ring = erase while moving hand.
🖐️ 5 fingersHold open palm about 0.7s to open the touchless tools menu.
✊ FistPause drawing; hold to close the tools menu.
TOOLS & HOW TO USE

Open palm → choose Colors / Brush Size / Eraser Size / Auto Straight / Undo / Save. Point and hold on a menu tile to select. For size controls, point at a number.

For accurate gestures, keep your full hand in frame, with good lighting and fingers clearly separated.
